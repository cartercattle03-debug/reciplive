# Decisions

- 2026-10-14: RecipLive built from Savannah 2.0 as its own app (separate from HerdLive for now; may join it later). Same stack and look as HerdLive.
- 2026-10-14: Customer logins - an owner (e.g. Lehman) gets a read-only view of only their cows and report.
- 2026-10-14: Sheet2 and Sort Sheet were scratch tabs - not brought over.
- 2026-10-14: Customer PDF = Print / Save as PDF from Customer Search for now; emailing it from the app is for later.
- 2026-10-14: Helper columns are not stored - all worked out live (src/lib/recip.ts), checked row-for-row against the workbook.
- 2026-10-14: Attempt # = her records in batch order, then entry order (the sheet's formula had a fixed "row 2" in it).
- 2026-10-14: "Confirm Delivery Updates" (in How To Use but never written) isn't needed - delivery saves as it's picked.
- 2026-10-14: Add transfers is a 10-line grid (+10 more lines, paste a column to fill down, one Add for all) - batches run 40-100+ transfers.
- 2026-10-14: Manager login = view only: sees every page, no changes, no import, no print/download buttons. Only the owner saves.
- 2026-10-14: Red ✕ to delete beside every Transfers line (asks first, Undo keeps a copy); red ✕ clears a line on the Add transfers grid.
- 2026-10-14: Open Inventory gets a Cull tick box (Cull checked = her record turns Culled, dated) and an Edit pop-up for her latest record.
- 2026-10-14: Master Archive: each cow's purchase, every transfer / Pass, every preg check, delivery, cull and sale in order - built live from the records (new CowEvent table holds cull dates). Also at the bottom of Cow Lookup.
- 2026-10-14: Year box at the top; a new year (2027) starts a fresh slate on Dashboard, Transfers, 75+ Day, Breakdowns, Customer Search and Open Inventory.
- 2026-10-14: Open Inventory by year: add new cows, tick to Cull or Move to next year's opens, red X takes a gone cow off the list. A Nov/Dec transfer checked Open in Jan/Feb falls to the new year's opens.
- 2026-10-14: Preg Check columns: Group, Cow ID, Days Bred, Result, Check Date, Status, Due Date, Notes (Mating / Owner show when pointing at the Cow ID).
- 2026-10-14: Each preg check result gets its own Check Date - today when picked, cleared if the result is taken back off. The page-wide Date box is gone.
- 2026-10-14: A transfer typed as 54 / 968h goes on the cow already on file as 054 / 968H (only when exactly one fits), so it takes her off the open list.
- 2026-10-14: Preg Check: Mating column (editable) after Status; Due Date editable (typed date wins, Days Bred follows; blank goes back to transfer + 276); + Add cow puts a missed cow on the sheet as a Pending transfer so she counts in the totals.
- 2026-10-14: RecipLive colors: sky blue #0369a1 headings, copper #b8732e accent, dark headings (to read apart from HerdLive's navy / rust).
- 2026-10-14: Table rows: light gray every other row + thin charcoal line under each row (look D).
- 2026-10-14: Page tabs on a solid blue bar, white bold text; the open page is a white tab with a copper line.
- 2026-10-14: Cow IDs black on every page; the Group column is the blue one.
- 2026-10-14: Preg Check: Status next to Result; only the Result column is cream-shaded (others still click to edit); Notes twice as wide (also on Transfers and Open Inventory).
- 2026-10-14: Preg Check Result is three tap buttons (Probable / Safe / Open; tap again to clear) - built for the iPad at the chute.
- 2026-10-14: Preg Check: no column shading; Result buttons in their own colors (yellow / bright green / bright red); Safe and Open text brighter everywhere.
- 2026-10-14: Customer portal gets their own breakdowns (by Group, Embryo Type, Company, Grade, with the grids) and a Year box; owner-only 'View as <owner>' (/my?as=) shows exactly what a customer sees.
- 2026-10-14: Culled cows count in the pregnancy rate: as Open (Safe if confirmed Safe before the cull; a culled Pass stays a Pass). Overall rate on the workbook data: 48.2% the sheet's way -> 41.8%.
- 2026-10-14: A culled cow counts Open even if checked Safe first (6019) - a failed pregnancy. Only a culled Pass (stays Pass) or a cow already Delivered (stays Safe) differ.
- 2026-10-14: Breakdown and Dashboard tables show a Culled column (counted against Open) and Pregnant / Checked (e.g. 1/3) beside the rate, so the math is on screen.
- 2026-10-14: Billing for confirmed 75-day pregnancies: Ready to bill (Safe records not on an open invoice; culled never), Build invoices (one per owner, one price from settings), price changeable for every line or one line, adjustment lines (minus = discount), void keeps the number and frees the cows, QuickBooks Online CSV import file per invoice or all not yet exported. Billed cows are matched by record and by cow + group + mating, so a re-import can't bill twice.
- 2026-10-14: Billing picks by owner + group(s); everything shown comes up ticked; untick what isn't going. Unticked cows stay on Ready to bill marked Held back (date + invoice she was left off), shaded yellow with a count at the top, until billed. Notes editable on each line.
- 2026-10-14: Page tabs in four labeled groups (Daily, Reports, Cow records, Office) that wrap - all visible, no sideways scroll; each tab a clear button, the open one white with a copper ring.
- 2026-10-14: Preg checks match their record by group + Cow ID (mating only breaks a tie, loosely) - the old sheet lost three Buck 6-19 Safes (6300, 6118, 6139) because the 9/16 chute sheet typed the mating differently. The import now puts such records forward to their last check; the Dashboard shows a "didn't catch up" card to fix them on the live site (tick, Fix, Undo-able).
- 2026-10-14: Invoice terms default to Due on receipt (due date = invoice date; QuickBooks Terms 'Due on receipt'). Any number of days still works (Net N).
- 2026-10-14: Terms box is a choice: Due on Receipt (default), Net 10 / 15 / 30 / 45 / 60. The 'didn't catch up' card also shows on Billing.
- 2026-10-14: Billing: 'Why isn't a cow here?' box - each of her records in plain words (billed on #, culled, not Safe, preg checks).
- 2026-10-14: Invoice header on Billing: logo upload (shrunk in the browser, kept on the ranch record), business name, address lines, phone, email - prints on every invoice. Price per pregnancy box never prints; each line's Amount and the Total do.
- 2026-10-14: Invoices show who is billed under Bill To - Ranch Name, Location, Email Address. Typed once per owner on Lists → Owners; new invoices copy it, and that owner's Open invoices fill in any blank; each invoice can still be changed on its own.
- 2026-10-14: Manager logins have no access to billing - no Billing tab, the Billing and invoice pages send them to the Dashboard, the QuickBooks download refuses them, and Lists hides owners' billing info.
- 2026-10-14: Manager logins see nothing about money or customer contact info: no Master Sheet or Master Archive (tabs gone, pages send them to the Dashboard); Cow Lookup hides purchase / cost / loan / sale / transaction # and the archive's Price column; Open Inventory hides the add-cows (purchase) form; logins and owner billing info stay owner-only.
- 2026-10-14: List headings never scroll off the screen: each list's box is sized to the screen under the pinned header (HeaderSize sets --hdr, .list-box), so scrolling the page stops with the headings just under the tabs; the customer list and one cow's archive got the same box.
- 2026-10-14: Preg Check: a cow whose status is already Probable gets only Safe and Open buttons (the save refuses Probable for her too); a Probable typed for her earlier still shows so it can be tapped off.
- 2026-10-14: Preg Check totals split when first-check and 75-day cows are on the list together: First check (not yet Probable: % Probable / Safe / Open) and 75-day check (already Probable: % Safe / Open). A Pending cow keeps the Safe button - now and then her first check is at 75 days.
- 2026-10-14: A culled cow shows as Open (what her transfer came to - `shownStatus`) on every list and report, customer portal included, so a customer never sees "Culled" against their cows; the Culled column is gone from Dashboard and breakdowns (they were already in Open). She stays stored as Culled; Master Archive keeps the cull with its date, Master Sheet Final Destination still says Culled (owner only), and Transfers can still filter Culled.
- 2026-10-14: Customer view: a tip above their list says any column heading can be clicked to sort (again to flip). Not printed.
- 2026-10-14: Transfers bulk add: Status is Pending, Pass or Culled (a cow added Culled gets her dated cull). The group's Transfer Date shows and fills itself in - from Lists, or read from the label (7-31 = 7/31 of the Year box year; 7-31-25 carries its own); + New group puts a new group on Lists with that date. A group that already has a date keeps it (change it on Lists).
- 2026-10-14: An invoice keeps each cow as she was billed; a cow whose record changed since (culled, no longer Safe, deleted) is marked in red on the invoice, with a banner, and the Billing list shows "(N changed since)" - so the invoice and the customer's live count can be squared up (Buck 6-19: invoiced 7, customer view 5). Nothing changes on the invoice by itself.
- 2026-10-15: Account page wording for Manager logins matches what they can see: view only, no billing, costs, sales or customer contact info.
- 2026-10-15: Customer page: By Mating, By Sire and By Donor (matings read as Sire x Donor; spellings that differ only in spaces / slashes / dots / dashes are one), most transfers first, Pass never counts. Under 3 checked the rate stays blank ("under 3 checked") - too few to judge a mating. Customer page only; not on the staff Breakdowns.
- 2026-10-15: Lost after Probable and Loss % on Breakdowns (staff and customer) and the customer's By Mating / Sire / Donor: a record checked Probable (or culled while Probable) that ends Open is lost, one that ends Safe held; Loss % = Lost / (Lost + Held). A culled Probable is a loss no matter how it's cut (Carter). On By Mating, under 3 decided the % stays blank.
- 2026-10-15: Loss % shows only once 3 or more Probables have had their 75-day answer, everywhere (Buck: 1 of 1 read 100%); under that it reads "under 3" and the Lost count still shows.
- 2026-10-15: Loss % taken back off (Carter: rather a fraction). Lost after Probable reads "1 of 10" - lost out of every cow checked Probable (held, lost or still waiting); the tooltip gives the split.
- 2026-10-15: A status key in words on the customer report (staff Customer Search and the customer's page, printed too), the customer's breakdowns and the staff Breakdowns: Probable = pregnant at the first preg check (about 42 days), Safe = confirmed pregnant at the final preg check (75 days), Open = not pregnant, Pending = not checked yet. Lost after Probable worded the same way.
- 2026-10-15: Lost after Probable: a cow checked Safe counts as having been Probable first (she had to be, to be checked Safe) - fills in groups whose first check came before the sheet's archive began (7/15/2026), e.g. Buck 5-21 now 1 of 14. A Safe cow later culled counts as lost (a culled Safe is a failed pregnancy). Times Probable on records is unchanged (`wasProbable` is separate from `everProbable`).
- 2026-10-15: Breakdown grids (Fresh / Frozen by company and group, and the others) show lost after Probable in each cell ("lost 1 of 10") under the rate, and an All column per row - a company across every group - to see which company loses more later on. Staff and customer grids.
